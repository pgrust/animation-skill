# PARALLEL PIPELINE

This generated project starts from the parallel-pipeline archetype: eight
persistent work items cross three stages in three depth lanes. One item
demonstrates the complete operation before later items overlap.

Replace the teaching sentence, event table, labels, and manifest facts in
`build.py`. Keep the oblique composition, density, state responses, four-beat
pacing, and assertions until your own mechanism gives you a specific reason to
change them.

Stage names are attached to the front faces of their stage blocks. Work-item
IDs are attached to the colored top plates and move with them. Keep semantic
labels parented to their owners; use global camera-facing text only for the
title, caption, or final takeaway.

From `modes/blender/`, run:

```bash
projects/parallel-pipeline/render.sh check
projects/parallel-pipeline/render.sh preview 1 98 220 330
projects/parallel-pipeline/render.sh timeline
projects/parallel-pipeline/render.sh final
projects/parallel-pipeline/render.sh contact-sheet
```

Read [the visual-direction guide](../../references/visual-direction.md) before
reducing the object count, flattening the camera, or removing a story beat.
Generated output stays under `output/` and does not enter Git.
